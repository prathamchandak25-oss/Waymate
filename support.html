Frequently Asked

How do I book a ride on Waymate? +
Simply navigate to the "Find a Ride" page, enter your departure and destination points along with your travel date. Browse the available drivers heading your way, select the one that fits your schedule, and tap "Book Seat."
Are the drivers verified for safety? +
Yes! Safety is our top priority. We verify every user's identity via government-issued ID and phone number. We also maintain a strict two-way rating and review system so you can review a driver's track record before booking.
What happens if my driver cancels? +
If a driver cancels your ride, you will receive an immediate full refund to your original payment method. We will also help you quickly find an alternative ride heading to your destination.
How do I offer a ride and get paid? +
Go to "Offer a Ride," input your trip details, vehicle information, and the contribution amount you're requesting per seat. Once the ride is complete, funds are automatically transferred to your linked bank account within 48 hours.

Still need help?
